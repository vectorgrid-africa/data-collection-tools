SOP 1.1: Site & Household Selection
===================================

This quick reference summarizes the site selection criteria, household enumeration protocol, and replacement rules from **VectorGrid SOP 1.1**.

Sentinel Site Selection Criteria (Table 5)
------------------------------------------
Participating observatory nodes select sentinel sites based on standardized epidemiological and logistical benchmarks:

* **Malaria & Arbovirus Endemicity:** Documented historical transmission of *Plasmodium falciparum* or arboviral vectors.
* **Ecological Diversity:** Representative coverage of rural agricultural landscapes, peri-urban settlements, and major aquatic breeding ecologies.
* **Year-Round Accessibility:** Safe access for field vehicles or motorbikes during both wet and dry seasons.
* **Community Engagement:** Established administrative liaison with village leadership and community consent.

Household Selection & Enumeration Protocol (Table 6)
----------------------------------------------------

1. **Sampling Frame:**
   All residential dwellings in the sentinel village are mapped during initial site reconnaissance to establish the total enumeration roster.

2. **Random Walk Selection:**
   Starting from a central community landmark (school, clinic, or village office), enumerators cast a random spin to establish direction, sampling every *k*-th house along the transect until the target sample size is achieved.

3. **Spatial Distribution:**
   Selected households must be spaced at least 30 to 50 meters apart to ensure independent mosquito trapping ranges and avoid competition between traps.

4. **Informed Consent & Baseline Placard:**
   Enumerators obtain written or witnessed verbal consent from the head of household. A weather-resistant identification placard bearing the unique Household ID (e.g. ``HH-001``) is affixed near the main entrance.

Reserve Household Replacement Rules (Section 5.0 to 6.0)
--------------------------------------------------------
* **Withdrawal or Relocation:** If an enrolled household relocates, dismantles the dwelling, or withdraws consent, field teams record the transition on ``HH-UPD`` (Household Status Update).
* **Pre-Enrolled Reserve List:** Dwellings are replaced strictly from the pre-selected reserve household roster in the same village cluster.
* **ID Continuity:** The new household receives a distinct unique Household ID; never reassign a retired Household ID to a new dwelling.
