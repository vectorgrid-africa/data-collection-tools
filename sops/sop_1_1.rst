SOP 1.1: Site & Household Selection
===================================

This quick reference summarises the site selection criteria, household selection protocol, and replacement rules from **VectorGrid SOP 1.1**.

Node Structure & Sentinel Site Criteria (Tables 1 and 2)
--------------------------------------------------------
For each observatory node, ten sites at the administrative level of village or neighbourhood are selected: one monthly sentinel site and nine supplementary sites (sampled biannually, with at least four supplementary sites sampled per year).

Participating observatory nodes select sampling sites following the five criteria in Table 2:

* **Logistics & Feasibility:** Year-round access for monthly sentinel sites (wet and dry season access for supplementary sites), with a minimum size of 10 households per site.
* **Minimum Distance Between Sites:** Minimum 2 km between sites (optimum ~5 km) to exceed typical *Anopheles* and *Aedes* dispersal ranges.
* **Ecological Diversity:** Broad representation of agro-ecological, environmental, or climatic conditions across rural and urban landscapes.
* **Human & Zoonotic Disease Risk:** Areas of documented vector-borne disease transmission or elevated One Health risk.
* **Security & Community Acceptability:** Safety of field teams and community willingness to participate.

Household Selection Protocol (Table 3)
--------------------------------------

1. **Random Selection (Preferred):**
   Use a random number generator on a census list of households in the sentinel village. If no census list is available, generate approximately 20 random GPS points in QGIS spaced at least 100 metres apart and sample the nearest accessible houses.

2. **Haphazard Selection (Secondary):**
   If random selection is impractical, field teams may select households haphazardly while maintaining geographical distribution.

3. **Spatial Distribution:**
   Selected households must be at least 100 metres distance from one another to ensure independent mosquito trapping ranges and avoid competition between traps.

4. **Informed Consent & Logistics:**
   Field teams obtain written or witnessed verbal consent from the head of household and verify that selected dwellings remain accessible in wet and dry seasons.

Reserve Household Replacement Rules
-----------------------------------
* **Withdrawal or Relocation:** If an enrolled household relocates, dismantles the dwelling, or withdraws consent, field teams record the change on ``HH-UPD`` (Household Status Update).
* **Pre-Enrolled Reserve List:** Dwellings are replaced strictly from the pre-selected reserve household roster in the same village cluster.
* **ID Continuity:** The new household receives a distinct unique Household ID; never reassign a retired Household ID to a new dwelling.
