SOP 3.1: Larval Survey & Water Analysis
=======================================

This quick reference summarizes the water physicochemical analysis and voucher archiving protocol from **VectorGrid SOP Task 3.1**.

Table 6: Water Physico-Chemical Parameters
------------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :widths: 25 20 30 25
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Parameter
     - Standard Units
     - Instrument
     - Calibration
   * - **Water Temperature**
     - °C
     - Multi-parameter digital probe
     - Daily verification
   * - **pH Level**
     - 0.0 to 14.0
     - Digital pH meter with electrode
     - Weekly (pH 4.0, 7.0, 10.0)
   * - **Conductivity**
     - μS/cm
     - Electrical conductivity sensor
     - Monthly standard
   * - **Dissolved Oxygen**
     - mg/L
     - Optical DO sensor
     - Before each field round
   * - **Total Dissolved Solids**
     - mg/L
     - Combined TDS meter
     - Monthly standard

Table 13: Specimen Preservation Guidelines

.. list-table::

   * - Preservation Target

     - Downstream Identifier
   * - **Live Rearing**

   * - **Molecular / PCR**
