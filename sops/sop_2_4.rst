SOP 2.4: Mosquito Processing & Biobank Barcoding
================================================

This quick reference summarizes the cold chain and specimen plate barcoding protocol from **VectorGrid SOP Task 2.4**.

Barcode Convention
------------------
Every storage box, plate, and tube rack must carry a machine-readable barcode following the standard observatory naming convention:

.. code-block:: text

   [Country Code][Node Code] [Box Number]

Format specification:

* **Country Code:** 2-letter ISO country code representing the partner country (such as ``TZ``, ``KE``, ``MZ``, ``ZA``, or ``MG``).
* **Node Code:** 2 to 4-letter unique observatory node identifier assigned to the field station (for example, ``01``, ``02``, or local node code).
* **Box Number:** 4-digit sequential box or rack number, zero-padded (such as ``0001``, ``0002``).

Generic Examples:

* ``TZ01 0001``: Participating observatory node in Tanzania, Box 0001
* ``KE01 0001``: Participating observatory node in Kenya, Box 0001
* ``MZ01 0002``: Participating observatory node in Mozambique, Box 0002

Plate Allocation Rules
----------------------
* **96-Well Microplate Layout:** Rows A to H and Columns 1 to 12.
* **Specimen Packing:**
  * Individual: 1 mosquito per well or tube.
  * Pool: Up to 10 mosquitoes of the same taxon, sex, and physiological status per tube.
* **Temperature and Preservation:** Store plates at -20°C (field base) or -80°C (central repository) with color-indicating silica gel desiccant.
