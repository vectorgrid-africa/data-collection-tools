Form Downloads
==============

ODK forms for ODK Central (XLSForm ``.xlsx`` and XForm ``.xml``) and paper layouts (``.docx``) for training and review. The ODK forms provide a reference implementation of the VectorGrid SOPs: each node sets its own country and location lists (the distributed forms include demonstration lists for testing) and preserves the SOP variables, field names and codes.

The paper layouts are printable documents for training and review. They list the variables each form captures, using the terms of the VectorGrid SOPs. The ODK forms are laid out for entry on a phone or tablet, so some questions, the screen order and the grouping can differ from the paper layouts.

Setting Up ODK Central
----------------------

The registration and update forms share three ODK Central entity lists:

.. list-table::
   :widths: 18 22 22 38
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Entity List
     - Created by
     - Updated by
     - Selected in
   * - ``households``
     - ``HH-REG``
     - ``HH-UPD``
     - ``TRAP-REG``, ``SPEC-SRT``, ``SPEC-DIS``, ``HH-BEH``, ``ENV-OBS``
   * - ``traps``
     - ``TRAP-REG``
     - ``TRAP-UPD``
     - ``SPEC-SRT``, ``SPEC-DIS``
   * - ``habitats``
     - ``HAB-REG``
     - ``HAB-UPD``
     - ``HAB-SRV``

1. Create a project in ODK Central.
2. Under **Entities**, create the lists ``households``, ``traps`` and ``habitats``, and turn on **auto-approve** so new records are available at once.
3. Upload the forms in this order, so each entity list exists before a form uses it:

   1. ``HH-REG``, ``HH-UPD``
   2. ``TRAP-REG``, ``TRAP-UPD``
   3. ``SPEC-SRT``, ``SPEC-DIS``, ``SPEC-HND``
   4. ``HH-BEH``, ``ENV-OBS``, ``ENV-EXT``
   5. ``HAB-REG``, ``HAB-UPD``, ``HAB-SRV``

4. Check that each form's ``households.csv``, ``traps.csv`` or ``habitats.csv`` attachment shows as linked to the entity list of the same name.
5. Test each form as a draft on ODK Collect first. Enketo web preview has limited entity support.
6. Use ODK Central's submission review workflow so incoming records are checked and approved by a second team member before export.
7. Schedule regular backups of your ODK Central server to protect your node's primary data.
