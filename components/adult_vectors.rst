Adult Vector Surveillance
=========================

Adult Vector Surveillance monitors mosquito abundance, species composition, physiological feeding status, parity rates, and indoor and outdoor temperature and humidity across sentinel observatories.

Scope & Objectives
------------------
- Standardise fixed and mobile trapping deployments across indoor and peridomestic outdoor sites.
- Track trap operational status updates, maintenance, battery replacements, and retirements.
- Differentiate morning and evening collection windows (for example, BG-Sentinel day/night portions).
- Morphologically separate *Anopheles gambiae s.l.*, *Anopheles funestus group*, and culicines.
- Perform fresh ovarian tracheole coiling dissections to determine parity rates and physiological age.
- Record nightly temperature, humidity, and data logger readings matching adult trap collections.
- Document extreme weather events (floods, cyclones, droughts) and evaluate surveillance interruptions.
