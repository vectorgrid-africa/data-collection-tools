Adult Vector Collection & Lab Sorting
=====================================

Adult Vector Collection & Lab Sorting monitors mosquito abundance, species composition, physiological feeding status, parity rates, environmental microclimate, and cold-chain specimen biobanking across sentinel observatories.

Scope & Objectives
------------------
- Standardize fixed and mobile trapping deployments across indoor and peridomestic outdoor sites.
- Maintain an operational status ledger tracking trap maintenance, battery replacements, and retirements.
- Differentiate morning and evening collection windows (for example, BG-Sentinel day/night portions).
- Morphologically separate *Anopheles gambiae s.l.*, *Anopheles funestus group*, and culicines.
- Perform fresh ovarian tracheole coiling dissections to determine parity rates and physiological age.
- Record nightly microclimate parameters and data logger telemetry matching adult trap collections.
- Document extreme climate shocks (floods, cyclones, droughts) and evaluate surveillance interruptions.
- Maintain cold-chain specimen biobanking under standard 96-well plate barcoding.
