Adult Mosquito Sampling
=======================

Adult mosquitoes are collected with CDC light traps and BG-Sentinel traps at the selected households, with DN-Mini traps or human landing catches during biannual sampling, and by indoor and outdoor resting collections. The forms record each trap and collection, the weather on the sampling night, and the sorting, preservation and transfer of the mosquitoes to the laboratory.

Scope & Objectives
------------------
- Standardise trap deployments indoors and in peridomestic outdoor sites (within 10 m of the house) and at other outdoor sites.
- Track trap status (active, malfunctioned, retired) and the reason for each change.
- Run BG-Sentinel traps for 24 hours with separate night and day collection bags.
- Sample human-mosquito contact hour by hour, indoors and outdoors, with volunteers in DN-Mini traps or by human landing catch, and record where people sleep or stay awake at night, with and without bed nets (including children under 5).
- Identify mosquitoes morphologically to the lowest reliable level (species complex where possible), and count *Anopheles* females by physiological status (unfed, partly fed, fully fed, semi-gravid, gravid).
- Perform fresh ovarian tracheole dissections to determine parity rates.
- Record weather, temperature, humidity and data logger readings for adult trapping nights.
- Document extreme weather events (floods, storms, droughts and others) and their effect on trapping.
- Keep a chain of custody for every sample shipment from the field to the laboratory.

Associated Forms
----------------
- :doc:`../forms/trap_reg`: Trap registration: method, CO\ :sub:`2` bait, indoor or outdoor placement, and the household or outdoor site. Creates the ``traps`` entity record.
- :doc:`../forms/trap_upd`: Trap status updates for maintenance, malfunction or retirement.
- :doc:`../forms/spec_srt`: Collection timing and validity, counts by species, sex and physiological status, tube packing and bycatch.
- :doc:`../forms/spec_dis`: Fresh dissection: midgut, spermatheca, parity, Christophers egg stage, sac stage, dilatations and wing length.
- :doc:`../forms/spec_hnd`: Sample shipment dispatch, cold chain, manifest and chain-of-custody sign-off.
- :doc:`../forms/hh_beh`: Biannual human-mosquito contact sampling (DN-Mini or HLC): net use indoors and outdoors, volunteer shifts and hourly catches.
- :doc:`../forms/env_obs`: Weather, rainfall, temperature and humidity readings and data logger records for trapping nights.
- :doc:`../forms/env_ext`: Extreme weather event type, severity, impacts and trapping continuity.
