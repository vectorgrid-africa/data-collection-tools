Larval Sampling
===============

Larval surveys locate, describe and sample aquatic habitats for mosquito larvae and pupae. They are done in the Supplementary Sites in the rainy and dry seasons; for *Aedes*, water-holding containers in houses and compounds are also sampled.

Scope & Objectives
------------------
- Register aquatic habitats with a GPS point and classify them by type (codes 1 to 22), origin (natural or artificial) and permanence.
- Track habitat status changes (seasonal drying, flooding, drainage, destruction) between surveys.
- At each visit, measure habitat size (length, width, perimeter, surface area, depth), shade, vegetation, algae, biofilm and canopy cover.
- Measure *in situ* water quality where a meter is available (temperature, pH, conductivity, salinity, dissolved oxygen, TDS).
- Take a standard 10 dips per habitat where its size allows, recording the number completed and the reason if fewer.
- Count early instars (L1-L2), L3, L4 and pupae by taxon, alongside aquatic predators, in each dip.
- Survey *Aedes* breeding containers within households and compounds.
- Assign vial IDs that link adults reared from larvae back to the habitat sample.
