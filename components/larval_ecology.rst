Larval & Aquatic Habitat Ecology
================================

Larval & Aquatic Habitat Ecology monitors natural and artificial aquatic breeding habitats, water quality parameters, dipping effort, aquatic predator communities, and larval specimen preservation across observatory localities.

Scope & Objectives
------------------
- Record GPS locations and classify breeding site types (swamps, puddles, burrow pits, artificial containers).
- Track habitat condition changes (seasonal drying, flooding, drainage, destruction) between dipping rounds.
- Measure water surface dimensions, perimeter, depth, shade, and vegetation or algal cover.
- Measure *in situ* water quality parameters (temperature, pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, TDS).
- Standardise dipping effort scaled to habitat perimeter and surface area.
- Count instars (L1-L2, L3, L4) and pupae, alongside aquatic predators (dragonflies, diving beetles).
- Assign sample vial IDs linking reared adults to the original aquatic collection site.
