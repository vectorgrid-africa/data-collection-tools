Household Surveillance
======================

Household Surveillance establishes the community census population, architectural vulnerability factors, bed net coverage, domestic animal proximity, human sleeping patterns, and baseline environmental exposure around homes.

Scope & Objectives
------------------
- Enumerate household occupants by age cohort (<5 years, 5 to 15 years, >15 years) and gender.
- Characterize house construction materials (roof, walls, floor, open eaves, screening).
- Quantify insecticide-treated net (ITN/LLIN) ownership, net condition, and hanging status.
- Audit longitudinal structural changes, bed net replacements, and resident mobility over time.
- Quantify human nocturnal activity, evening outdoor gathering, and age-specific net utilization.
- Assess proximity to animal sheds, latrines, and nearby aquatic breeding sites.

Associated Forms
----------------
- :doc:`../forms/hh_reg`: Baseline household registration, architectural characteristics, and occupant room census.
- :doc:`../forms/hh_upd`: Periodic household status update for dwelling modifications, occupant mobility, and net acquisitions.
